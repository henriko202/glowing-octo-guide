# Sub-Processor List

**Controller:** NEANDERTECH SERVICOS DE TECNOLOGIA LTDA **Service:** Link Into The SSI

This document lists all third-party organisations that process personal data on behalf of Link Into The SSI as data
controller. It is maintained in accordance with GDPR Art. 28 (3)(e) and equivalent obligations under UK GDPR, LGPD, and
applicable US state law.
