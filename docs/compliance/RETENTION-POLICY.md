# Data Retention Policy

**Controller:** NEANDERTECH SERVICOS DE TECNOLOGIA LTDA **Service:** Link Into The SSI

This document states the retention period for each category of personal data processed by Link Into The SSI, the legal
or business justification for that period, and the enforcement mechanism. It should be read alongside the ROPA (
`DATA-INVENTORY.md`) and the Sub-Processor List (`SUBPROCESSORS.md`).

> **Implementation note:** Retention periods are configurable constants in `convex/privacy/retention.ts`. The cron
> schedules that drive automated enforcement are in `convex/crons.ts` and documented in `docs/PLATFORM.md` §13 and §13B.

### AI features — OpenRouter

`posts.suggestedReaction` and `users.commentLanguage` follow the account lifecycle: retained for the life of the account
and removed in the account-deletion cascade (they are part of the `posts` / `users` records above). The industry
suggestion (ROPA Activity 13 (c)) stores nothing of its own: its only output is the `users.industry` code it fills while
that field is empty, which, like the `users.profileEditedAt` stamp that stops the automatic fill, is part of the `users`
record (life of the account, included in the DSR export, deleted in the cascade). Prompts sent to the inference provider
are **not stored** by Link Into The SSI. The generated comment draft is stored on the requesting
user's own rows (`userPostState.commentDraft`, reloaded by the comment box; the **first** draft for a post also lands in
that user's `userInteractions` log as an `ai_draft` entry) and follows the account lifecycle: retained for the life of
the account, included in the DSR export, deleted in the account-deletion cascade. Inference runs via **OpenRouter**,
which routes only to providers constrained in code to **zero data retention** + **no training**, so the
providers do not retain prompts either. **Confirm the ZDR / no-training terms in OpenRouter's DPA (and that they flow
down to its providers) [bracketed].**

### Landing funnel counter (first-party)

The landing's sign-up funnel counter (ROPA Activity 18) keeps two tables. `landingVisitors` rows hold a keyed daily hash
of the visitor's IP address and user agent, used only to count each visitor once per UTC day. The daily
`prune landing visitors` cron deletes every row from before the current UTC day, so no row outlives 48 hours: the
"deleted within two days" the public Privacy Policy and Cookie & Storage Policy promise. `landingMetrics` holds only
per-day integer totals, which identify no one, and is kept. The counter stores nothing in the visitor's browser.
The sign-in counters that follow those clicks through LinkedIn (`authMetrics`) are the same kind of per-day totals,
keyed by day and step, never by a person, and are kept too.

### Analytics, error monitoring and browser storage

Sentry, PostHog and DataFast hold their data on their own systems (ROPA Activities 14 and 17); Link Into The SSI stores
none of it in `convex/schema.ts`. Each processor keeps it for the period set in its dashboard or plan: configure the
shortest workable window [bracketed — record the configured periods]. What these tools keep in the browser:

- **Sentry** (app): `sentryReplaySession` in `sessionStorage`, cleared when the tab closes.
- **PostHog** (app): before consent, at most `__ph_opt_in_out_<key>` in `localStorage` (the choice, no identifier).
  After `analytics` consent, the `ph_<key>_posthog` cookie (1 year) and matching `localStorage` / `sessionStorage`
  items, deleted on withdrawal or sign-out.
- **DataFast, app** (only after `analytics` consent): the `datafast_visitor_id`, `datafast_visitor_first_seen_at` and
  `datafast_visitor_session_count` cookies (1 year) and the `datafast_session_id` and `datafast_session_start` cookies
  (30 minutes), deleted on withdrawal or sign-out.
- **DataFast, landing** (cookieless): `datafast_visitor_id`, `datafast_session_id` and `datafast_session_start` in
  `sessionStorage`, cleared when the tab closes.
- **DataFast, both sites:** `datafast_event_queue` in `localStorage` holds events not yet sent and empties once they are
  sent.
- **DataFast ids at payment** (only after `analytics` consent): the visitor and session ids also ride in the Stripe
  Checkout Session metadata when the member subscribes or buys a featured slot (Activity 6), where Stripe retains them
  under its own retention policy.
- **Cloudflare Web Analytics:** stores nothing in the browser.

## User Rights and Overrides

- Users can request hard deletion of their account at any time (Settings → Privacy & Data → Delete Account), triggering
  the 30-day grace-period flow described above.
- Users can request a data export at any time (Settings → Privacy & Data → Export My Data); the export file is available
  for 7 days.
- Users can opt out of the 3-day activity digest at any time in Settings → Emails, or with the one-click unsubscribe
  link carried by every digest (which works without logging in); re-enabling requires a login. The ranking display has
  its own opt-out in the same Settings section. Both changes append `auditLogs` rows.
- Dormant users who log in within the 30-day grace window see the cancellation screen and can cancel scheduled deletion
  via `cancelAccountDeletion`.
